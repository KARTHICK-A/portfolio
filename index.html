U1 DUAL-FUEL ENGINE CONTROL UNIT (LPG/CNG) AUTOMOTIVE · MARINE-VERIFIED [+]

Indigenous low-cost ECU architecture converting IC engines to dual-fuel LPG/CNG — including a marine engine retrofit. Real-time engine sensing (MAP, EGT, engine speed) through a precision ADC with in-house calibration across firmware versions; fuel and injector control logic implemented in-house. V1 verified on a running marine engine under operating load.

STM32 ▸ ESP32 ▸ ARDUINO MEGA ▸ ADS1115 ▸ MAP/EGT/RPM ▸ CAN ▸ EMBEDDED C ▸ STM32CubeIDE

U2 INJECTOR MULTIPLIER POWER ELECTRONICS · 4→16 CH [+]

Power-electronics module multiplying 4 ECU injector channels to drive up to 16 injectors — so large multi-cylinder engines can run on a low-channel, low-cost ECU. Driver stages rated 12 V / 8 A peak per channel with MOSFET switching, flyback and reverse-polarity protection, on a custom PCB.

MOSFET DRIVE ▸ FLYBACK PROT ▸ REV-POLARITY PROT ▸ CUSTOM PCB

U3 ACOUSTIC VEHICLE ALERTING SYSTEM EV SAFETY · PROTOTYPE [+]

Pedestrian-safety prototype for quiet EVs — a speed-dependent acoustic warning system where tone and volume rise with vehicle speed, extending engine-sensing work into EV safety hardware.

ESP32-S3 ▸ PWM AUDIO ▸ ANALOG SPEED INPUT ▸ C/C++ ▸ PLATFORMIO

U4 OIL CONTROL UNIT AUTOMOTIVE · 2 HW VERSIONS [+]

Electronic oil-pump controller for regulated oil delivery. Designed, manufactured and verified two hardware versions on custom PCBs — ESP8266 control, PWM output, MOSFET/TIP122 power stage with protection.

ESP8266 ▸ PWM ▸ MOSFET/TIP122 STAGE ▸ CUSTOM PCB

U5 TRANSFORMER CONDITION MONITORING INDO TECH · POC DONE [+]

IoT logger for Indo Tech Transformers (power-equipment manufacturer): pressure, voltage, current, temperature and running hours acquired with RTC timestamping, local storage and cloud sync. POC completed with on-site validation.

ESP ▸ PT100 ▸ ZMPT101B ▸ SCT013 ▸ RTC ▸ SD LOG ▸ HTTP/JSON ▸ P10 LED

U6 COLD CHAMBER MONITORING SYSTEM DELPHI TVS · DELIVERED [+]
