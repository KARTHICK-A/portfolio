U1 DUAL-FUEL ENGINE CONTROL UNIT (LPG/CNG) ★ AUTOMOTIVE · MARINE-VERIFIED [+]
PROBLEM
Converting IC engines to LPG/CNG needs an ECU that senses and doses fuel in real time — imported units cost too much for retrofit markets.
CONSTRAINTS
Low-cost BOM · engine-bay conditions · in-house sensor calibration (MAP, EGT, RPM via precision ADC) · marine retrofit variant.
OUTCOME
V1 verified on a running marine engine under operating load; calibration held across firmware revisions.

STM32 ▸ ESP32 ▸ ARDUINO MEGA ▸ ADS1115 ▸ MAP/EGT/RPM ▸ CAN ▸ EMBEDDED C ▸ STM32CubeIDE

U2 INJECTOR MULTIPLIER ★ POWER ELECTRONICS · 4→16 CH [+]
PROBLEM
Affordable ECUs ship with 4 injector channels; large multi-cylinder engines need up to 16.
CONSTRAINTS
12 V / 8 A peak per channel · inductive loads demand flyback and reverse-polarity protection · custom PCB.
OUTCOME
4 channels multiplied to 16 — big engines run on a low-channel, low-cost ECU.

MOSFET DRIVE ▸ FLYBACK PROT ▸ REV-POLARITY PROT ▸ CUSTOM PCB

U3 ACOUSTIC VEHICLE ALERTING SYSTEM EV SAFETY · PROTOTYPE [+]
PROBLEM
Quiet EVs are a pedestrian hazard; regulation is moving toward mandatory acoustic alerting.
CONSTRAINTS
Tone and volume must track vehicle speed continuously from an analog speed input.
OUTCOME
Working prototype on ESP32-S3 — engine-sensing work extended into EV safety hardware.

ESP32-S3 ▸ PWM AUDIO ▸ ANALOG SPEED INPUT ▸ C/C++ ▸ PLATFORMIO

U4 OIL CONTROL UNIT AUTOMOTIVE · 2 HW VERSIONS [+]
PROBLEM
Oil pumps need regulated electronic delivery in place of fixed mechanical feed.
CONSTRAINTS
Compact custom PCB · MOSFET/TIP122 power stage with protection · PWM-controlled output.
OUTCOME
Two hardware versions designed, manufactured and verified.

ESP8266 ▸ PWM ▸ MOSFET/TIP122 STAGE ▸ CUSTOM PCB

U5 TRANSFORMER CONDITION MONITORING INDO TECH · POC DONE [+]

CONSTRAINTS

OUTCOME
POC completed for Indo Tech Transformers with on-site validation.

ESP ▸ PT100 ▸ ZMPT101B ▸ SCT013 ▸ RTC ▸ SD LOG ▸ HTTP/JSON ▸ P10 LED

U6 COLD CHAMBER MONITORING SYSTEM ★ DELPHI TVS · DELIVERED [+]
