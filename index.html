Dual-Fuel Engine Control Unit (LPG/CNG) Automotive · Spark Invotech

An indigenous, low-cost ECU architecture that converts IC engines to dual-fuel LPG/CNG — including a marine engine retrofit. Integrated real-time engine sensing (MAP, EGT, engine speed) through a precision ADC with in-house calibration, and implemented the fuel and injector control logic. V1 was verified on a running marine engine under operating load.

STM32 · ESP32 · Arduino Mega · ADS1115 · MAP / EGT / RPM sensors · CAN · Embedded C · STM32CubeIDE

Injector Multiplier Power electronics · ECU expansion

A power-electronics module that multiplies 4 ECU injector channels to drive up to 16 injectors — letting large multi-cylinder engines run on a low-channel, low-cost ECU. Driver stages rated 12 V / 8 A peak per channel, with MOSFET switching, flyback and reverse-polarity protection on a custom PCB.

MOSFET driver stages · flyback protection · reverse-polarity protection · custom PCB

Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System (AVAS) EV safety · Prototype

A pedestrian-safety prototype for quiet electric vehicles: a speed-dependent acoustic warning system where tone and volume rise with vehicle speed — extending engine-sensing work into EV safety hardware.

ESP32-S3 · PWM audio generation · analog speed input · Embedded C/C++ · PlatformIO

Oil Control Unit Automotive · Two hardware versions

An electronic oil pump controller for regulated oil delivery. Designed, manufactured and verified two hardware versions on custom PCBs — ESP8266 control, PWM output, and a MOSFET/TIP122 power stage with protection.

ESP8266 · PWM · MOSFET / TIP122 power stage · custom PCB

Transformer Condition Monitoring Industrial IoT · Indo Tech Transformers

An IoT logger for a power-equipment manufacturer, acquiring pressure, voltage, current, temperature and running hours with RTC timestamping, local storage and cloud sync. Proof of concept completed with on-site validation.

ESP · PT100 · ZMPT101B · SCT013 · RTC · SD logging · HTTP/JSON cloud APIs · P10 LED display

Cold Chamber Monitoring System Industrial IoT · Delphi TVS · Delivered

A 3-node distributed data logger for temperature profiling from +50 °C down to −20 °C at a diesel fuel-injection systems manufacturer. Architected a command/ACK protocol with 3-retry logic and session persistence across power cycles; validated on-site before handover.

ESP32 · RS485 half-duplex (DE/RE) · LoRa E32 (~200 m deployed) · SPIFFS logging · Python PC interface
